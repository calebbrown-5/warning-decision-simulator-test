VERIFICATION · TRAINING ANALYSIS

  
Uses all qualifying reports in the prepared case, including reports hidden from trainees. Actual LSR occurrence time and relocated coordinates are used; display/reveal delays do not affect verification. This is an IEM COW-inspired approximation, not official verification or a trainee grade.

  

  
Pre-start reports are always historical context, not trainee misses. Reports in the first five minutes are flagged as limited opportunity even with no exclusion. The unadjusted column is the exercise-window reference, not official historical COW. Warnings still active are pending, not false alarms. Separate SVR/TOR results are shown below the combined overview.

  
Results appear after a prepared case and an exercise session are loaded.

  

  
BY WARNING TYPE · SVR / TOR

  
SVR considers qualifying hail and damaging wind reports; TOR considers tornado reports. A severe thunderstorm warning does not verify a tornado report. Zero TOR warnings means TOR FAR is unavailable, not 0%.

  

  

   Advanced metrics · polygon area, report footprint & lead-time distributions
   
The footprint is the sampled portion of each warning's original issued polygon lying within a radius of qualifying LSRs that matched that warning at occurrence time. It is not an observed damage swath, SVS time-integrated area, or exact IEM COW areal verification. Spatial samples use WFO-scale approximate distances. Overlapping reports are counted once per polygon.

   

   

   

   
Area shrink/expansion compares the first and last active issued polygons. Report buffer radius affects only the footprint diagnostic, never POD, FAR, CSI, or matching. Report lead time uses the earliest issued, compatible, covering warning. Median, quartiles, and warning-first leads are diagnostics, not an aggregate grade.

  

  
WARNING VERIFICATION

  
REPORT MATCHES AND EXCLUSIONS

  

  

  

  
Defaults: wind ≥58 mph, hail ≥1.00 in, 1 km warning polygon buffer. Wind-damage reports can verify SVRs without a numeric gust. Report matching uses active polygons at occurrence time, including issued SVS geometry updates and cancellations. Approximate original-polygon report-buffer footprint is available under Advanced metrics. IEM historical warning comparisons and exact COW parity are not implemented. Interpret report-based verification alongside instructor judgment.
